## Downloaded at run time, never in this tree

- On-device AI models (speech recognition, voice clean-up, separation, face models…): each from its author's own repository, under its own licence (`apps/web/src/ondevice/manifest.ts` names every file, its source and checksum).
- Fonts outside the bundled set (`apps/web/src/fonts/remote-fonts.json`, SIL OFL 1.1, from github.com/google/fonts).
- Sticker files, from their publishers (catalogs in `apps/web/public/stickers/`).
- AI generation runs on the services you connect with your own key (fal.ai, kie.ai, ArgoLink, Anthropic…): their terms apply to what they make.

## Kept out of this tree

Content whose licence does not allow redistribution (for example LottieFiles animations, music and sound catalogs whose licence forbids re-hosting, a personal graphics library) is not part of the source and is not in a build made from it (`PUBLIC_RELEASE`, the default here: `desktop/content-licenses.mjs strip`).
