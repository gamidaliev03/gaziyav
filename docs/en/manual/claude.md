# Claude inside Gaziyav

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Editing by saying what you want. Claude works in your open project with the editor's own operations; every step lands on the timeline you see and can be undone.

## Connect

1. Install Gaziyav and the Claude app for Mac (or Claude Code). Anthropic's help pages state that desktop extensions are available to all users on Claude Desktop.
2. Gaziyav → Settings → **AI** → **Claude** → **Connect Claude**. One press hands Claude everything this Mac has: Claude for Mac opens the Gaziyav extension and installs it; for Claude Code the editing skill and the server are added. The status line says what is connected; **Connect again** repairs it.
3. Open a project in Gaziyav. Talk to Claude in its own window. If Gaziyav is closed, Claude starts it in the background.

When Gaziyav updates, Claude's skill is refreshed on the next start.

## What it costs

Gaziyav sells nothing for this and needs no API key: the connector is part of the app and runs on your Mac. Claude's own messages count toward the plan of your Claude app, as any chat does. Paid generation (a voice, a picture, a video, lipsync) runs only on your own key and is priced before it starts ([Extras](extras.md)).

## What to ask

- «Remove the pauses and the ums in the first clip.»
- «Captions in a bold style, two lines at most.»
- «A title "Day one" at 0:02, and make it bigger.»
- «Make it vertical for Reels and keep it under 30 seconds.»
- «Lower my music under the voice and fade it out at the end.»
- «Put this YouTube piece after the second clip: <link>, from 1:10 to 1:25.»
- «Find the scenes of this film; move scene 4 before scene 2; give me YouTube chapters.»
- «Look at the cut at 0:05 and 0:12 and tell me what's off.»
- «Export it for TikTok.» · «Post it to YouTube Shorts» (it asks before posting).

What Claude can reach: projects, import (your files, a YouTube piece, web pictures), cutting by words and times, trims, moves, speed, freeze, reverse, captions, titles and text designs, transitions, effects, filters, auto colour, keyframes and camera moves, sound levels and voice clean-up, scenes and chapters, looking at frames, export per platform, posting after your yes, and the paid generation on your key.

## Undo

Every call is ordinary editing: one <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> per step in Gaziyav, or ask Claude to undo. You can also change anything it did by hand — Claude reads your hand edits on its next call and keeps them.

## What it cannot do

- See pictures you paste into the chat: save them to a folder and ask Claude to import them.
- Work without Gaziyav: it edits the open project of the app on this Mac (the browser edition has no connector).
- Post without your yes, or spend on paid models beyond the limit you set.
- Use a music or sticker library: this edition has none — give it your own files.
