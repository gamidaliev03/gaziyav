# Sound

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Levels, fades, music under a voice, a clean voice, music and sound effects from the library.

**How.**
- Volume of the selected clips: <kbd>⌥⌘.</kbd> up, <kbd>⌥⌘,</kbd> down. Exact dB, pan, fade in and fade out are in the inspector.
- Music under the voice: select the music → **duck under voice** in the inspector. The music dips wherever someone speaks.
- Voice tools in the inspector: denoise, voice EQ (voice / podcast), compressor.
- Free on your Mac ([on-device AI](ondevice-ai.md)): **enhance voice** (noise, hum, room echo), **separate voice and music** into two clips, **voice changer**.
- Detach a clip's sound to its own track: <kbd>⌃⇧S</kbd>. Mute a clip: <kbd>⇧V</kbd>.
- Loudness: the meter at the right of the timeline reads LUFS. Export can bring the sound to a platform's level.

**The library.** In the Mac app the Audio tab has 1,515 music tracks and 7,765 sound effects; the browser edition has 311 and 2,514. A track or sound downloads in the background the first time you need it and is kept in the cache: from the author's page on Hugging Face, Mixkit items from Mixkit's own site, each checked by SHA-256; the 1,204 tracks and 5,251 sounds added in 1.0.61 (CC0) come from the original author's asset host. Your own music and sounds come in through Media and appear under «Yours».

**Good to know.** With linkage on (<kbd>`</kbd>), a clip's sound moves and trims with its picture.
