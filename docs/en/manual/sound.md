# Sound

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Levels, fades, music under a voice, a clean voice.

**How.**
- Volume of the selected clips: <kbd>⌥⌘.</kbd> up, <kbd>⌥⌘,</kbd> down; exact dB, pan, fade in and fade out in the inspector.
- Music under the voice: select the music → **duck under voice** in the inspector; it dips wherever someone speaks.
- Voice tools in the inspector: denoise, voice EQ (voice / podcast), compressor.
- Free on your Mac ([on-device AI](ondevice-ai.md)): **enhance voice** (noise, hum, room), **separate voice and music** into two clips, **voice changer**.
- Detach a clip's sound to its own track: <kbd>⌃⇧S</kbd>. Mute a clip: <kbd>⇧V</kbd>.
- Loudness: the meter at the right of the timeline reads LUFS; export can bring the sound to a platform's level.

**Rules that surprise people.**
- With linkage on (<kbd>`</kbd>) a clip's sound moves and trims with its picture.
- Your own music and sounds come in through Media («Yours» in the Audio tab).

**Limits.** No built-in music or sound-effect library in this edition.
