# Titles and text

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Titles, lower thirds and callouts: text on its own track above the picture.

**How.**
- A plain title at the playhead: <kbd>⌃T</kbd>. Type in the player or in the inspector.
- A designed title: the **Text** tab → drag a design onto the timeline, or double-click it to place it at the playhead.
- Style in the inspector: font, size, colour, background, stroke, shadow, position; an entrance and an exit animation (fade, pop, rise, drop, slam).
- Copy a title's look to others: <kbd>⇧⌘C</kbd>, select them, <kbd>⇧⌘V</kbd> (Copy / Paste Attributes).

**Good to know.**
- A title is a clip like any other: it moves, trims and copies with the same gestures ([the timeline](timeline.md)).
- Typing in a title stays fast: 33 redraws per letter, down from 1,039.
- To check text against the social-app frame and text zones, turn on the markup: <kbd>⇧⌘;</kbd> ([the player](player.md)).

**Motion graphics.** Motion-graphics layers are rendered with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/license), which has its own licence: free for individuals, organisations of up to three people and non-profits; larger companies need Remotion's company licence.
