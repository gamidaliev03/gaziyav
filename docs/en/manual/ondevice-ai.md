# Free on-device AI

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** The AI an editor needs every day, on your own Mac: no key, no account, no per-use price.

**What there is.**

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Speech recognition | Captions from speech, remove pauses and filler words, word timing |
| Enhance voice | Takes noise, hum and room out of a voice |
| Separate voice and music | The clip's voice and music as two clips (a faster variant on Apple silicon; a beat checker for songs) |
| Voice changer | The clip's speech in another voice, timing and intonation kept |
| CleanUp | Removes an object, a person or a caption from a photo or a clip and fills the background |
| Retouch | Sharper eyes and smoother skin on the person's own face |
| Sound recognition for Claude | Lets [Claude](claude.md) ask how a stretch sounds and name what is heard |

Built in, nothing to install: remove or blur the background, enhance, upscale 2× / 4×, deflicker, auto colour, split into scenes, make vertical (the face kept in frame), beat markers, tracking (blur or pixelate a moving face or object).

**How.**
- Settings → **AI** lists the features, each with its download size and the memory it needs; **Download** one, or **Download all**.
- Or just use it: the first time a feature is needed, Gaziyav offers to fetch its model.
- On a clip: the inspector and the clip's menu; or ask [Claude](claude.md) in words.

**Rules that surprise people.**
- Models come once from Hugging Face (a mirror, hf-mirror.com, when it cannot be reached; a few from the model's original publisher), every file checked by SHA-256. After that they work offline.
- Your media never leaves the Mac for these.

**Limits.** Apple silicon Macs. The heavy ones want memory; a Mac under 16 GB is not offered the heaviest. Not in the browser edition.
