# Effects, transitions, filters

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** The look and the movement between shots.

**How.**
- **Effects** tab: drag an effect onto a clip. The inspector opens on it: its settings are in the clip's **Effects** tab ([The inspector](inspector.md)).
- **Transitions** tab: drag a transition onto a cut between two clips; drag its edge to change its length.
- **Filters** tab: colour looks; drag one onto a clip. It is shown at the top of the clip's **Color** tab — its name, its strength, **Remove**.
- **Generators** tab: solids, gradients, moving backgrounds and overlays that land as clips of their own ([Generators](generators.md)).
- Copy one clip's effects and settings to others: <kbd>⇧⌘C</kbd> → select → <kbd>⇧⌘V</kbd>.
- Free on your Mac: remove or blur the background, enhance, upscale 2× / 4×, deflicker, retouch, remove an object ([on-device AI](ondevice-ai.md)).
- Masks: ellipse, rectangle, heart, split, with feather — in the clip's **Effects** tab.

**Rules that surprise people.**
- Effects never change your source files.
