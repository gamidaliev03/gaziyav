# Colour

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Correcting a shot and giving the film one look.

**How.** Everything about a clip's colour is in the inspector's **Color** tab ([The inspector](inspector.md)):
- At the top, the filter on the clip, if it has one: its name, its strength, **Remove**. A look is picked in the **Filters** tab ([Effects, transitions, filters](effects.md)).
- The colour correction by hand — exposure, contrast, saturation, temperature and more; the everyday sliders lie open.
- **AI color correction** (auto colour): one press, free, on your Mac. **Color match**: the colour of another shot.
- Same colour on many clips: <kbd>⇧⌘C</kbd> → select → <kbd>⇧⌘V</kbd>.

**Rules that surprise people.**
- The player and the thumbnails stay neutral in every interface [look](looks.md), so colour is judged the same everywhere.
- Export sets the colour space explicitly ([Export](export.md)).

**Limits.** In the [browser edition](browser.md) the Color tab has the filter and the correction by hand; auto colour is in the clip's right-click menu and the timeline toolbar's **More tools**; Color match needs the Mac app.
