# The player and its guides

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Watching the cut, and checking where things sit in the frame.

**How.**
- Play / pause <kbd>Space</kbd>; shuttle <kbd>J</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> (press again to go faster); a frame <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, ten frames <kbd>⇧←</kbd> <kbd>⇧→</kbd>.
- Previous / next cut <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>; start / end <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd>; go to a timecode <kbd>G</kbd>; loop <kbd>⌘L</kbd>.
- Zoom the player <kbd>⌥⇧=</kbd> <kbd>⌥⇧-</kbd>, fit <kbd>⌥⇧Z</kbd>; full screen <kbd>⇧⌘F</kbd>.
- Guides <kbd>⌘;</kbd>, rulers <kbd>⌥⌘R</kbd>.
- **Markup** <kbd>⇧⌘;</kbd>: the social frame for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and VK Clips, TV action / title safe, text zones, thirds.
- Move, scale and rotate the selected clip in the player by its handles.

**Good to know.**
- Markup is never exported: frames exported with it on and off are identical (90 of 90).
