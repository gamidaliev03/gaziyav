# Markers

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Pins on the timeline: beats, notes, places to come back to; snapping catches on them.

**How.**
- Add at the playhead <kbd>M</kbd>; in another colour <kbd>⌥M</kbd>.
- Next / previous <kbd>⇧M</kbd> / <kbd>⌥⇧M</kbd>.
- Mark a beat while the music plays: <kbd>⌘J</kbd>. Beat markers can also be found for you on a music clip (the [on-device](ondevice-ai.md) beat finder).
- In / out range: <kbd>I</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>, clear <kbd>⌥X</kbd>, go to <kbd>⇧I</kbd> <kbd>⇧O</kbd>, play in to out <kbd>/</kbd>; mark the clip's range <kbd>⇧X</kbd>.
- Lift the in–out range (leave a gap) <kbd>;</kbd>; extract it (close the gap) <kbd>'</kbd>.

**Rules that surprise people.** Lift and extract act on every track inside the range.
