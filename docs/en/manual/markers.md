# Markers

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Pins on the timeline for beats, notes and places to come back to. Clips snap to them.

**How.**
- Add at the playhead <kbd>M</kbd>; in another colour <kbd>⌥M</kbd>.
- Next / previous <kbd>⇧M</kbd> / <kbd>⌥⇧M</kbd>.
- Mark a beat while the music plays: <kbd>⌘J</kbd>. Gaziyav can also find the beats of a music clip for you ([on-device AI](ondevice-ai.md)).
- In / out range: <kbd>I</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>, clear <kbd>⌥X</kbd>, go to <kbd>⇧I</kbd> <kbd>⇧O</kbd>, play in to out <kbd>/</kbd>; mark the clip's range <kbd>⇧X</kbd>.
- The in–out band has no labels. Drag an edge to move the in or out point; drag the middle to move the whole piece. This works on the timeline, on a Media tile, in the source monitor and in the [link window](link.md). The small **×** clears the range (on a Media tile too); the source monitor has a **Clear** button.
- In the source monitor and over a Media tile, <kbd>I</kbd> / <kbd>O</kbd> mark where the line is: the line on the tile and the source monitor share one playhead.
- Lift the in–out range (leave a gap) <kbd>;</kbd>; extract it (close the gap) <kbd>'</kbd>.

**Good to know.** Lift and extract act on every track inside the range.
