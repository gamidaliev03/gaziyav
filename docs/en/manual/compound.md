# Compound clips

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Treating a group of clips as one clip — a nested sequence you can move, trim, colour and reuse.

**How.**
- Make: select clips → <kbd>⌥G</kbd>.
- Open: select it → <kbd>⌘↓</kbd>. Back out: <kbd>⌘↑</kbd> — the parent's playhead, zoom and scroll come back, level by level.
- Take apart: <kbd>⌥⇧G</kbd>.
- Group without nesting: <kbd>⌘G</kbd>, ungroup <kbd>⇧⌘G</kbd>.

**Rules that surprise people.**
- Duplicates of a compound clip share its contents: change one inside, all change.
- Older projects that had several timelines open with the extra ones as compound clips in Media: double-click to open, drag to place. Nothing is converted until you edit, and a «before joining» version is kept.
