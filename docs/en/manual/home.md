# Home and projects

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Home lists your projects. One project is one timeline and its media.

**How.**
- New project: **New project** on Home, or <kbd>⌘N</kbd> anywhere. Pick the frame: 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), 16:9 (YouTube), 1:1 or 4:5 (feeds). You can change it later in the project's settings.
- Open: click a project card. Back to Home: <kbd>⌥⌘Q</kbd> (All Projects). Close the project: <kbd>⌘W</kbd>.
- Rename: click the project's name at the top of the editor to open its menu.

**Good to know.**
- You never have to save: every edit is on disk within a quarter of a second (at most three seconds while you keep editing). A drag becomes a change only when you drop it.
- Projects and their media stay on your disk, in Gaziyav's folder. Nothing is uploaded.
- On APFS disks an imported file is cloned, not copied: a 57 MB clip takes no extra space.

**Limits.** One project has one timeline. To nest parts of it, use [compound clips](compound.md).
