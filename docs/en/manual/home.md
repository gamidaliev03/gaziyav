# Home and projects

[← Manual](README.md)

**What it is for.** Home lists your projects; one project is one timeline and its media.

**How.**
- New project: **New project** on Home, or <kbd>⌘N</kbd> anywhere. Pick the frame: 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), 16:9 (YouTube), 1:1 or 4:5 (feeds). The frame can be changed later in the project's settings.
- Open: click a project card. Back to Home: <kbd>⌥⌘Q</kbd> (All Projects). Close the project: <kbd>⌘W</kbd>.
- Rename: the project's name at the top of the editor opens its menu.

**Rules that surprise people.**
- There is no Save button you must remember: every edit is on disk within a quarter of a second (at most three seconds while you keep editing). A drag in progress is not a change until you drop it.
- Projects and their media live on your disk, in Gaziyav's folder; nothing is uploaded.
- An imported file is cloned, not copied, on APFS disks: a 57 MB clip costs 0 extra bytes.

**Limits.** One project has one timeline (nest parts of it as [compound clips](compound.md)).
