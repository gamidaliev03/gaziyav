# Your first five minutes: a 9:16 reel

**English** · [Русский](../ru/first-five-minutes.md)

You need one or two of your own clips. No account, no key. AI is optional and comes at the end.

## 1. A project (20 seconds)

On Home, click **New project**. Drop your clips anywhere on the window, or press <kbd>⌘I</kbd> to pick them. They appear in **Media**.

## 2. Clips in order (1 minute)

Drag the first clip from Media onto the timeline, then the next one right after it. The main track is magnetic (<kbd>P</kbd> switches it), so clips close up and leave no gap.

- <kbd>Space</kbd> plays. <kbd>J</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> play backwards, stop, play forwards (press again to go faster).
- Find the moment you want to keep. <kbd>Q</kbd> cuts off everything before the playhead, <kbd>W</kbd> everything after it.
- <kbd>⌘B</kbd> splits at the playhead. Select the piece you don't want and press <kbd>⇧⌫</kbd>: it goes, and the gap closes.
- <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes the last edit. Every edit is one step.

## 3. Vertical (20 seconds)

Right-click a clip → **Make vertical** → **9:16**. Gaziyav reframes each clip to 9:16 and keeps its face (or main subject) in frame with smoothed keyframes. One <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes it. Turn on the guides with <kbd>⇧⌘;</kbd> to see where the buttons of TikTok, Reels and Shorts will cover your picture.

## 4. Words on screen (1 minute)

- **A title:** open the **Text** tab, drag a style onto the timeline above your clips, double-click the text in the player and type.
- **Captions:** open **Captions** and generate them. Speech is recognised on your Mac. The first time, the app downloads the speech model and shows its size.

## 5. Sound (1 minute)

Take a track from **Audio → Music** or a sound from **Audio → Sound effects** (311 tracks and 2,514 sounds; each downloads the first time you use it). Or drop your own file on the window, like the clips. Put it on the timeline under your clips. Volume and fades are in the inspector on the right; for a quick fade, drag the small handles at the clip's corners.

## 6. Export (30 seconds)

Press <kbd>⌘E</kbd>. The defaults suit the project (1080 × 1920, H.264, your project's frame rate). Click **Export** and choose where to save. There is no watermark.

## Next: let Claude edit

Connect Claude (**Settings → AI → Claude → Connect Claude**) and write «remove the pauses and add bold captions». Claude edits this same project step by step, and <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes each step. See [Claude inside Gaziyav](manual/claude.md).

Optional, on your own fal.ai key, for cents: to make a face say new words, select the clip and press <kbd>⌥L</kbd>. See [Lipsync](lipsync.md).
