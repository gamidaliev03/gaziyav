# Your first five minutes: a 9:16 reel

**English** · [Русский](../ru/first-five-minutes.md)

You need one or two clips of your own. No account, no key — AI is optional and comes last.

## 1. A project (20 seconds)

On Home, click **New project**. Drop your clips anywhere on the window, or press <kbd>⌘I</kbd> to pick them. They appear in **Media**.

## 2. Clips in order (1 minute)

Drag the first clip from Media onto the timeline. Drag the next one right after it — the main track is magnetic (<kbd>P</kbd> switches it), so clips close up and never leave a gap.

- Play with <kbd>Space</kbd>; <kbd>J</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> shuttle backwards, stop, forwards (press again to go faster).
- Find the moment you want to keep, then <kbd>Q</kbd> cuts everything before the playhead off the clip and <kbd>W</kbd> everything after it.
- <kbd>⌘B</kbd> splits at the playhead; select the piece you do not want and press <kbd>⇧⌫</kbd> to delete it and close the gap.
- Made a mistake? <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>. Every edit is exactly one step.

## 3. Vertical (20 seconds)

Right-click a clip → **Make vertical** → **9:16**. Gaziyav reframes for 9:16 and keeps each clip's face (or its main subject) in frame with smoothed keyframes — one <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes it. Turn on the guides with <kbd>⇧⌘;</kbd> to see where TikTok's, Reels' and Shorts' buttons will cover your picture.

## 4. Words on screen (1 minute)

- **A title:** open the **Text** tab, drag a style onto the timeline above your clips, double-click the text in the player to type.
- **Captions:** open **Captions** and generate them. Speech is recognised on your Mac — the first time, the app downloads the speech model and says how big it is.

## 5. Sound (1 minute)

Bring your own song or sound the same way as the clips (drop the file on the window), then drag it from Media under your clips. Gaziyav carries no music library of its own. Its volume and fades are in the inspector on the right; drag the small handles at the clip's corners for quick fades.

## 6. Export (30 seconds)

Press <kbd>⌘E</kbd>. The defaults suit the project's shape (1080 × 1920, H.264, your project's frame rate). Click **Export** and choose where to save. No watermark, ever.

## Then: let Claude do the next one

Connect Claude (**Settings → AI → Claude → Connect Claude**) and say «remove the pauses and add bold captions» — it edits this same project, step by step, every step undone with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>. See [Claude inside Gaziyav](manual/claude.md).

Optional, on your own fal.ai key and for cents: make a face say new words — select the clip, <kbd>⌥L</kbd>. See [Lipsync](lipsync.md).
