# Support the project

**English** · [Русский](../ru/support.md)

Gaziyav is free and stays free. Nothing here is required.

## Without spending anything

- **Star the repository** — it is how other people find it.
- **Show it to one person** who edits video, records a podcast or makes reels.
- **Report what broke**, with steps — every reproducible bug becomes a test.
- **Translate or fix the docs** — a pull request is welcome.
