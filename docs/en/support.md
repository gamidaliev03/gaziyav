# Support the project

**English** · [Русский](../ru/support.md)

Gaziyav is free and will stay free. None of this is required.

## Without spending anything

- **Star the repository.** That is how other people find it.
- **Show it to one person** who edits video, records a podcast or makes reels.
- **Report what broke**, with steps. Every bug we can repeat becomes a test.
- **Translate or fix the docs** in a pull request.
